Questions

Good to know.

Which mixer should I pick?

Both mixers cost the same. The Behringer X Air XR18 has 16 mic inputs and you run it from an iPad or laptop, so it suits full bands. The Allen & Heath CQ-12T has 10 inputs, a built-in touchscreen and Bluetooth for playlists. It is the simplest one to use, so it suits smaller acts. The calculator picks one for you.

Do I need the sub?

For talking, singing and acoustic music, the two QSC K8.2 speakers are usually enough. Add the sub if you have a kick drum, bass or dance music and want the low end to be felt.

How many channels do I need?

Count one channel for each vocal mic, instrument mic and DI box. The CQ-12T handles 10 and the XR18 handles 16. The calculator adds it up for you and shows how many channels you are using.

How long is a rental day?

A rental day covers one event. We settle pickup and return times when I confirm your booking. If you need the gear longer, each extra day is half price.

Do you deliver outside Boulder?

Ask. Usually yes, for a mileage fee.
